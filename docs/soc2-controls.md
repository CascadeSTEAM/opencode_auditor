## Compliance Tracking

Each audit item includes compliance tracking:

- **Risk ID**: AUDIT-YYYY-NNN
- **Likelihood/Impact**: High/Medium/Low
- **Control Objective**: CC6.1, CC6.8, CC7.2, etc.
- **Compliance Status**: Open -> In Progress -> Mitigated
